# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially:

- **Test reports.** Does it work on your PS4, remote and strip? Open an issue with
  your remote model, the IR protocol and codes, and what happened.
- **IR code tables** for other remotes (44-key, other brands).
- **Better colour matching** and smoother behaviour.
- **Support for other cheap controllers** (RF remotes, Bluetooth strips, smart plugs).

How to contribute: fork, create a branch, make your change, open a pull request.
Never commit `config.h` (Wi-Fi password). It is in `.gitignore`.
